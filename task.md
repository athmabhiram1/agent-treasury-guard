About Adevar Labs
Adevar Labs is a web3 security firm specializing in protocol audits with a focus on the Solana ecosystem. We work with teams that prioritize security as a core part of their development process, helping them assess the resilience of their systems. Our clients know that security is not a one-time event but an ongoing commitment. A good audit doesn’t just identify vulnerabilities, but challenges the way teams think about security. We exist to bring truth and clarity to an industry where security is often misunderstood or deprioritized.


Hackathon Track Scope
Adevar Labs is offering $20,000 in Pre Audit in-kind security services ($4,000 pre-audit value each across 5 teams) to Solana/Rust projects participating in Colosseum's Crypto World's Fair hackathon. Apply, answer a few questions, and tweet about it for a chance to receive a comprehensive pre-audit review from a SEAL-Certified audit firm.

Eligibility
Must have submitted to Colosseum Crypto World’s Fair

Open to any eligible Solana/Rust submission to Colosseum Crypto World's Fair

Solana/Rust submissions only

How it works:
Apply through this Superteam Earn bounty.

Answer a few short questions mentioned in the submission form about your project.

Tweet about your application (details/copy provided below).

Adevar Labs independently selects 5 recipients from eligible applicants based on set judging criteria.

Selected teams receive a full Pre-Audit service valued at $4,000 at absolutely no cost (in-kind service, no cash alternative).


Judging Criteria:
Codebase complexity and architectural clarity

Team technical readiness and repository completeness

Potential ecosystem impact and launch readiness

What each $4,000 Pre-Audit includes:
Scope: Automated AI static analysis paired with mandatory human security engineer review and false-positive triage.

Deliverables: Comprehensive written pre-audit report detailing prioritized vulnerability findings and remediation guidance.

Turnaround: Fast-track review delivered within standard pre-audit turnaround timeframe.

Remediation: One follow-up verification review included to confirm fix resolution.

Terms: Redeemable for up to 6 months post-announcement; non-transferable, in-kind service only (no cash alternative).

Tweet requirement:
Tweet about your application for this bounty and follow @AdevarLabs on X.

Suggested copy:

“Just submitted our project to the @AdevarLabs Security Sidetrack on @superteamearn for @Colosseum’s Crypto World’s Fair Hackathon

Excited for the chance to receive a full security Pre-Audit for our project.

[project link]”

Reward:
$20,000 total in in-kind security services (5 free Pre-Audits valued at $4,000 each), independently selected and awarded by Adevar Labs to 5 eligible Solana/Rust projects.


Timeline:

Bounty open: 23rd September 2026

Bounty closes: 12 October 2026 (alongside Crypto World's Fair submission deadline)

Recipients announced: Shortly after hackathon submission deadline
